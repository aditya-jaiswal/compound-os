# Acme Insights - positioning
**For** B2B data and analytics teams **who** find out about metric problems too late,
**Acme Insights is** an analytics platform with anomaly-based smart-alerts **that** cuts alert-to-action to under 5 minutes,
**unlike** threshold-only tools (Insighto) or query-first tools (Datenwerk) **we** combine anomaly detection with shareable views.
**Proof points:** Globex pilot (3 teams), Initech renewal on saved-views + alerts.
